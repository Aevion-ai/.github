# Aevion LLC

**Verifiable AI systems and deterministic control planes.**

Aevion builds evidence-first infrastructure for workflows where models may
propose or route work, while policy, provenance, receipts, and explicit human
authority govern consequential effects.

## Public focus

- **Evidence and receipts:** content-addressed artifacts, verification records,
  and replay-oriented workflows where implemented.
- **Human authority:** explicit `PASS`, `FAIL`, `UNKNOWN`, and `HOLD` states at
  consequential boundaries.
- **Agent systems:** bounded execution, qualification, provenance, and
  independent assessment.
- **Research:** formal methods, aerospace human factors, model evaluation, and
  reproducible security experiments.
